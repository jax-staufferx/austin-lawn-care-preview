MEET THE OWNER

Austin Bean

Austin Bean is the founder and CEO of Austin Lawn Care, a dynamic lawn care company dedicated to making yards look better. With four years of experience in the industry, Austin brings a wealth of knowledge and expertise to the table. Their journey in the industry began with their grandparents not being able to find a lawn care specialist to maintain their yard, where they honed their skills and developed a deep understanding of the nuances within the market. Driven by a passion for innovation and a commitment to excellence, Austin embarked on the entrepreneurial path to establish Austin Lawn Care. Their leadership has been instrumental in steering the company towards remarkable growth and success.
